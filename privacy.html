Privacy Policy

Data Protection & Security Protocol

Our Privacy Mission

To provide a secure sanctuary for your trading data, ensuring that your intellectual property and edge remain strictly your own through elite encryption.

1. Information We Collect

We collect essential information required to maintain your trading terminal. This includes your email address via Firebase Authentication for identity verification and the trade-specific data that you manually input into your journal.

2. Payment & Financial Security

Your financial privacy is our priority. All transactions are handled securely by Instamojo Payments. Traders IQ never sees, stores, or processes your credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank account details. All payment processing complies with PCI-DSS standards.

3. Data Security (Firebase)

All your trading history is stored using industry-standard encryption on Google Firebase Cloud. Your data is linked strictly to your unique User ID (UID). We implement strict security rules to ensure that only you can read or write to your personal database.

4. Third-Party Policy

Traders IQ follows a strict "Zero-Share" policy. We do not sell or trade your trading performance or journal entries with third-party marketers, hedge funds, or brokerage firms. Your edge is your own.

5. Data Deletion

You have the right to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated trade data. Once deleted, your logs cannot be recovered from our cloud servers.
