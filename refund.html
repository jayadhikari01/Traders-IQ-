Users have the absolute autonomy to cancel their premium membership or subscription plan at any point in time. There are no locking periods, permanent commitments, or hidden cancellation fee structures enforced.

Refund eligibility depends strictly on the absolute number of calendar days passed since initial plan activation or free trial configuration.

If you decide to cancel your subscription and officially file a refund claim via email within 10 calendar days from the date of sign-up/purchase, you will receive a 50% partial refund of the total base amount paid. The remaining 50% is retained to cover server costs, technical setup fees, payment gateway handling charges, and initial data provisioning.

If a cancellation is initialized or a refund application email is dispatched after 10 calendar days have elapsed, no refund will be issued under any circumstances. You will hold completely unrestricted access to all your dashboard features until the current billing cycle finishes, and no subsequent renewals will be charged.
